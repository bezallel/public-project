Financial Controls & Reconciliation Automation

  • Category: Financial Controls & Reconciliation Automation System
  • Technical Skills: Excel processing, JavaScript automation, data reconciliation, PDF generation

Overview: Designed and implemented a financial controls automation system that processes structured vendor invoice and purchase order data to detect billing discrepancies, enforce contractual compliance, and automate financial recovery workflows. The system reconciles invoice line items against agreed purchase terms, identifies overbilling and underbilling events, and generates structured audit-ready debit notes for review and supplier recovery processes.

Impact: Improved financial control accuracy by automating reconciliation workflows, reducing manual processing effort, and enabling faster identification and recovery of supplier overcharges, resulting in significant operational efficiency gains and measurable cost savings
